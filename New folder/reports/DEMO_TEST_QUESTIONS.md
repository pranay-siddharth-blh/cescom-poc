Every question on this page was asked against the live assistant on the VPN, generated 07 October 2026 from the latest test reports (nothing is hand-marked). A question is listed as **proven** only when it passed **all four** checks:

1. **Typed** — the figures recomputed independently from the locked star schema appear in the answer.
2. **Voice mode** — the same, plus a spoken answer with no screen marks that says the figure asked for.
3. **Spoken by voice A** (Windows Ravi: letters and short year forms, "L T four", "F Y twenty twenty-four twenty-five") — heard by the real speech-to-text and answered correctly.
4. **Spoken by voice B** (Windows Heera: words and long year forms, "financial year twenty twenty-four to twenty twenty-five").
