## Booklet p.42–44 (PDF 10, 7, 8) — Special Incentive Scheme / Discounted Energy Rate Scheme (DERS)
- Special Incentive Scheme (SIS): withdrawn w.e.f. 01 April 2025.
- DERS, A. Framework: eligible consumers opt in by written consent; charged Rs 5/unit for consumption above their base level. Runs until the Commission withdraws it.
  Eligible: HT-2a (industrial), HT-2b (commercial), HT-2c(i), HT-2c(ii), LT-3 (commercial, sanctioned load ≥ 50 kW), LT-5 (industrial, ≥ 50 kW).
- B. Base consumption:
  (a) Per Tariff Order 2021: monthly base = ESCOM consumption Apr-2019 to Mar-2021 excluding COVID period (Apr–Oct 2020); min six months' data; open-access/wheeled energy excluded.
  (b) Existing scheme consumers as on the effective date keep their base unchanged.
  (c) New opt-ins: i. two complete years FY2022-23 & FY2023-24 → average monthly of those two years; ii. otherwise average of ≥ 6 months' data; iii. new installations with < 6 months' data → 67 units/kVA (HT), 57 units/kW (LT), revised prospectively once 6 months' data exists; iv. load enhancement → enhanced portion at 67 units/kVA (HT) / 57 units/kW (LT) added to base, final; v. load reduction → base unchanged, no downward revision.
- C. Terms: i. each ESCOM designates an officer ≥ Superintending Engineer (Ele.) at Corporate Office to oversee, resolve disputes, approve base consumption (with sub-division offices); ii. written request to the sub-division office → forwarded to the designated officer; whole process ≤ 7 days; iii. email recommended between sub-division and Corporate Office; iv. opted consumers get an additional 20% contract demand / sanctioned load; v. consumption above base is not subject to FPPCA (recoverable or refundable), and ToD tariff does not apply to it (ToD applies up to the base monthly average); vi. DERS consumption above base is excluded when calculating additional security deposit; vii. must relinquish open access / wheeling (written undertaking); switching to OA forfeits the scheme; rejoining = new applicant. Existing participants as on the effective date are exempt for previously contracted OA quantum (KERC letter KERC/B7/14/531 dt 03.08.2021) but don't get the extra 20% contract demand.
- D. Period: extended for FY2025-26 at Rs 5/unit; FY2026-27 and FY2027-28 by separate orders.
